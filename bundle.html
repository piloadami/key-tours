Rio doesn't have one story — it has several that run through each other. The Colonial history and Afro-Brazilian foundations are in the city centre. The Atlantic Forest is one hour from the beach. Copacabana holds a century of modernist architecture and the apartment where bossa nova was invented. Lapa is a neighbourhood that turns into a city on Friday night.

A single five-hour tour follows one of those threads in depth. Two tours give you the contrast — and the contrast is the point.
